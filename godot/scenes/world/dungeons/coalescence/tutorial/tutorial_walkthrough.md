# Tutorial Walkthrough

## Summary

1. Pick up the item in the building to the right of the boulder.
2. Open your inventory and equip the new items. You must equip all of them to proceed.
3. Destroy the boulder with your Fireball ability.
4. Defeat the enemy that appears from the boulder.
5. Pick up the dropped item, equip it, and use it to recover health. This will make the boulder disappear.
6. Defeat the 3 enemies and collect the 3 items in the new area.
7. Defeat the miniboss by the purple circle at part of the area opposite the Archway.
8. Collect the miniboss' loot.
9. Open your inventory and equip new items as you see fit.
10. Defeat the new enemies that appear.
11. Defeat even more new enemies that appear, now with allies by your side.
12. Retreat from the big, bad boss by moving back to where you spawned in to exit the area.
13. Upon reaching the exit, the screen will fade to black. You're done!

## Intro

1. Walk forward towards the archway with the boulder.
 - WASD for directional controls, hold right-click and move the mouse to control where your character is facing.
2. Open the door of the building to your right.
 - Start by targeting the door either with a left mouse-click or using (TAB). Just be aware that if you use (TAB) the system will try to automatically pick the best target, and it's not perfect at doing so. If you keep pressing (TAB) it will cycle through the possible targets.
 - Once you target the door, then press (F) when you are close enough to it to open it.
 - Alternatively, if you don't currently have a target and are directly in front of the door, pressing (F) will automatically target and open the door.
3. Walk inside and pick up the item on the ground. Target it and then press (F).
 - Sometimes the targeting is finicky. If needed, press (Esc) to clear your target and then try targeting the item.

## Inventory

1. Open your inventory with (I).
2. Equip your newly acquired Mask, Weapon, and two Echoes by dragging and dropping them with your mouse from the right-most slots (backpack) to the left-most slots (equipment).
 - Each item can only be equipped in certain equipment slots. If you are dragging an item over a slot and your cursor changes to a red circle, it can not be equipped in that slot.
 - The Mask goes in the top-most center slot.
 - The Weapon goes in either the left- or right-most slots.
 - The Echoes can go in any of the bottom row of slots.
3. Close your inventory with (I).

## Destroy Boulder

1. Exit the building and return to the boulder.
 - Be careful not to move too fast and get stuck in the tree right outside the building. If this happens, you will be softlocked and need to restart.
2. Target the boulder.
 - If you're having trouble targeting the boulder, step back from it a little and try targeting it again. Make sure the whole thing is in the camera view. (This is a quirk with the targeting system currently)
3. Attack the boulder with your "Fireball" Echo to lower its health.
 - Your Echoes are bound to the number keys 1-4.
 - Press the key assigned to the red Fireball Echo to cast it and deal damage.
4. After 4 hits, an enemy should appear from within the boulder. (It takes a second because it is very dramatic)

## Defeat Enemy

1. Once the enemy settles and snaps to the ground, defeat it. (It should go down in one hit)
 - Target it and use the Fireball again to deal damage.
 - Again, the targeting may be finicky, so clear your target first if necessary and then target the enemy.
2. The enemy will drop an item on the ground.
3. Pick this item up, open your inventory, and equip this new Echo.
4. This Echo is a Healing Echo. Use its assigned key to recover some health.
 - PS. You can't see your health currently, but it works :)
5. After healing, the boulder should disappear and you can proceed forward.

## The Plaza

1. Your objective updates to defeat 3 enemies and collect 3 items within the new area. There are 3 designated locations that each include 1 enemy and 1 item. The order to clear these in does not matter.
 - PS. Due to some odd occlusion culling, the plaza objects may not snap into existence until you walk inwards a bit.
 - Also be careful moving through this space. The collision here is wonky. Some stuff will let you walk right through it, and some stuff might get you stuck and softlocked like the trees.
2. The first location is just to the left after passing through the archway, under the triangular pyramid structure (Southwest).
3. The second location is in the center of the area where the water pools into a small pond. The enemy is floating up near the middle, and the item is down by the base of the nearby tree.
 - Something is wrong with the player's gravity physics, so you will float around here. Don't worry about it for now, just pretend you are very scared of the water and also have magic levitating abilities.
 - The item under the tree is a little small, so targeting it can be kinda hard. No tricks to it though, just get close and target it as best you can.
 - There is another "enemy" standing by the shore. That's Jimmy - he's friendly and currently does nothing whatsoever. Feel free to wave and move on.
4. The third location is on the right-side of the area by some houses, not quite in the corner but along the right-most wall (Northeast).
5. Once you've defeated all 3 enemies and collected the 3 items near each of them, the objective should progress.

## Miniboss

1. Move to the part of the area opposite the Archway (ie. go North) to find a purple circle on the ground with a new enemy.
2. Defeat this miniboss and collect its loot.
3. Open your inventory and review your new items.
 - You are not required to equip anything here, but you can choose to do so if you like. You should have a near full set of equipment now.
4. Close your inventory when you are ready.


## Horde & Allies

1. Defeat the 3 enemies that spawn around the purple circle.
2. Once defeated, a new wave of enemies will spawn. Along with them will come 2 allies that stand on the purple circle.
3. Defeat the 5 enemies now while "assisted" by your allies.
 - At this moment, the allies don't do anything except look pretty. Eventually they will help by healing the player and occassionally attacking enemies.

## Escape

1. After defeating all the eneimes, a large boss will spawn at the nearby exit.
2. Retreat from the boss and escape the area. Run back through the Archway that had the boulder and keep running forward until you reach the approximate spot you spawned in at.
3. Upon reaching the exit, the screen will fade to black.
 - Currently the game will just hang on the black screen at this point. We'll update that to be something better at some point.
